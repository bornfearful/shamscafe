How it works

Four steps from hungry to fed.

Everything happens in a private order ticket inside our Discord server.

1

Join and open a ticket

Join the server and open an order ticket. A staff member and our ordering assistant are with you from there.

2

Fill in the order form

Your restaurant cart, delivery address and instructions. Add your mobile number only if you want text updates.

3

Pay and we place it

Once paid, we place the order with the restaurant's delivery service and share the live tracking link.

4

Track to your door

Follow the order in your ticket, or by text if you opted in, until it is delivered or ready for pickup.

Optional text updates

Know where your food is without opening Discord.

Enter a mobile number on the order form and Sham's Cafe texts you at the moments that matter: when the order is placed, when it is delivered or ready for pickup, and if it is ever cancelled and refunded.

  • Opt-in only. The field is blank by default and entirely optional.
  • One to three texts per order. No marketing, ever.
  • Reply STOP at any time to end texts, HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
