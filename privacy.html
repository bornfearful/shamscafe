Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

Sham's Cafe ("we", "us") operates a food delivery ordering service through our Discord server. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Order details you enter on the order form: restaurant cart, delivery address, delivery instructions, and your Discord account name.
  • Mobile phone number, only if you choose to enter one in the optional text updates field.
  • Payment records needed to process and refund your orders.

How we use it

  • To place and track your delivery order with the restaurant's delivery service.
  • To send you order status text messages, if you opted in.
  • To answer support requests and process refunds.

Text messaging

If you provide your mobile number, you consent to receive transactional order status text messages from Sham's Cafe. Message frequency varies, typically one to three messages per order. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as the SMS provider that transmits our messages, is permitted only to deliver those messages. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We share your delivery address and order contents with the restaurant and its delivery service so the order can be fulfilled. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Order records are kept as long as needed for support and accounting. You can ask us to delete your mobile number at any time by replying STOP or asking in our Discord server.

Contact

Reach Sham's Cafe in our Discord server: discord.gg/shamscafe.
