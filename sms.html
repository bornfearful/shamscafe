Order text updates

How Sham's Cafe order status text messages work, how you opt in, and how you opt out.

Program name: Sham's Cafe Order Updates

What we send

Sham's Cafe sends transactional text messages about food delivery orders you place with us. You may receive a text when your order is placed (with a tracking link), when it is delivered or ready for pickup, and, if it happens, when it is cancelled and refunded. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

How you opt in

You opt in by typing your mobile number into the optional "Mobile # for text updates" field on the order form inside our Discord server. The field is blank by default. Leaving it empty means you receive no texts. Entering a number is your consent to receive order status texts from Sham's Cafe for that order and later orders, until you opt out.

Consent language shown beside the field: "Optional. Enter your mobile number to get order status texts from Sham's Cafe when your order is placed, on the way and delivered. Msg frequency varies (1–3 texts per order). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy."

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. A typical order produces one to three messages. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

How you opt out

Reply STOP to any message from Sham's Cafe. You will receive one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to opt back in. You can also leave the mobile field empty on your next order, or ask in our Discord server and we will remove your number.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or ask in your order ticket in our Discord server (discord.gg/shamscafe).

Sample messages

Sham's Cafe — Order #55 placed Store: Super Pollo Deliver to: 123 Main St Total paid: $10.00 Track your order: (live tracking link) Reply STOP to opt out of these texts.
Sham's Cafe — Order #55 Your order has been delivered. Enjoy your food!
Sham's Cafe — Order #55 Your order was cancelled by the store. $10.00 has been refunded to your balance.

Privacy

Your mobile number is used only to send these order messages. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
