The service

Sham's Cafe takes food delivery orders through its Discord server and places them with restaurants' delivery services on your behalf. Delivery times, menus and availability depend on the restaurant and the courier.

Orders, payment and refunds

An order is confirmed once payment is received and the order has been placed with the restaurant. If a restaurant or courier cancels an order, the amount paid is refunded to your Sham's Cafe balance. Questions about an order go through your order ticket in our Discord server (discord.gg/shamscafe).

SMS terms

  1. Program. Sham's Cafe Order Updates sends order status text messages (order placed, delivered or ready for pickup, cancelled) to customers who opt in.
  2. Opt in. You opt in by entering your mobile number in the optional "Mobile # for text updates" field on the order form.
  3. Cancel. Reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply START to re-subscribe.
  4. Help. Reply HELP, or ask in your order ticket in our Discord server (discord.gg/shamscafe).
  5. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  6. Frequency and cost. Message frequency varies, typically one to three texts per order. Message and data rates may apply. Contact your carrier for details.
  7. Privacy. See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Contact

Reach Sham's Cafe in our Discord server: discord.gg/shamscafe.
